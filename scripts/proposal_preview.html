CampusFlow Enterprise Proposal Section 03 • AI Assessment & Digital Exam Integrity

03 Academic Intelligence & Examination Proctoring

CampusFlow combines cutting-edge generative AI with rigorous exam security to empower teachers and maintain uncompromising academic standards.

⚡

Gemini AI Question Studio

Autonomous, curriculum-aligned question generation powered by multi-model Google Gemini infrastructure. Teachers can formulate rich quizzes in seconds.

  • Teacher Notes & PDF Extraction: Upload PDF chapters or paste lecture notes; AI strictly restricts questions to the uploaded content.
  • Diverse Question Archetypes: Standard MCQs, CBSE Assertion & Reason, Numerical Computations, and HOTS Case Studies.
  • Cognitive Level Tuning: Select between Bloom's Recall, Balanced, or Analytical High-Order Thinking Skills.
  • Step-by-Step Explanations: Every question auto-generates pedagogical explanations for post-test student remediation.
  • One-Click Workflow: Review, fine-tune marks or options, and immediately publish to the active class division.
🛡️

Online Proctoring Exam Portal

A locked-down, distraction-free environment for institutional assessments with automated digital proctoring checks.

  • Mandatory Fullscreen Lockdown: Prevents opening background applications or split-screen notes.
  • Tab Switch Detector: Immediately detects window defocus or tab switching, pauses test, and issues audible warnings.
  • 3-Strike Auto-Disqualification: Upon the 3rd tab switch, the exam automatically locks and submits with a Disqualification Flag.
  • Status-Coded Question Palette: Green (Answered), Red (Unanswered), Purple (Marked for Review), Gray (Not Visited).
  • Proctoring Audit Trail: Logs student time, violation counts, and integrity rating for teacher inspection.
🚨

Proctoring Integrity Guaranteed for Board Standards

Every student submission embeds cryptographic timestamping, question response vectors, and tab-switch violation counters directly visible to teachers in the Pending Student Review Queue before official grade release.

Continuous Formative & Summative Evaluation Desk

8-Pending Review Queue

Teachers receive a prioritized queue of student submissions requiring attention, with automated score previews and remark inputs.

Question-Level Analysis

Detailed breakdown of class accuracy per question, identifying conceptual misconceptions across chapters.

CBSE CCE Report Cards

Automated compilation of scholastic and co-scholastic marks into official printable CBSE report cards with school seal.

High Availability AI Fallback Chain: Built with an intelligent model fallback chain (Gemini Flash Lite, Gemini 3.5 Flash, Gemini 3.6 Flash) ensuring zero downtime and sub-3-second response times even during peak school testing hours.

CampusFlow Enterprise Proposal Section 04 • Financial Operations, Payroll & Campus Utilities

04 Financial Operations & 7th CPC Payroll

CampusFlow digitizes institutional cash flow with an end-to-end fee accounting engine and an audit-compliant staff payroll system designed around Indian statutory requirements.

Fee Counter & Smart Cashier Desk

Streamline bursar operations with high-speed receipt generation and real-time ledger accounting:

  • Multi-Head Fee Structures: Tuition, Laboratory, Smart Class, Sports, Annual Library, and Transport.
  • Instant 80G Tax Receipts: Print official fee receipts with school logo, receipt number, parent PAN, and total amount converted into Indian Rupee words.
  • Flexible Installment Management: Term-wise, quarterly, or monthly schedules with automated late fee calculation.
  • Defaulters Tracking: Real-time filtering of students with overdue balances; one-click SMS/Email parent reminders.
  • Payment Modalities: Supports Cash, UPI, Cheque, NEFT/RTGS, and Online Gateway transactions.

7th CPC Staff Payroll & Biometrics

Eliminate spreadsheet errors with a fully automated, attendance-reconciled salary computation stack:

  • Biometric Attendance Integration: Tracks Total Working Days, Days Worked, Paid Leaves, and Loss of Pay (LOP).
  • Real-World Salary Stack: Basic Pay, Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), Transport Allowance (TA), Medical & Special Allowances.
  • Statutory Deductions: Automated EPF (12% of Basic+DA), Professional Tax (PT), and TDS Section 192 deductions.
  • Certified Printable Pay Slips: Official salary slips with bank details, UAN/PF numbers, Rupee words, and authorized signatory seals.
